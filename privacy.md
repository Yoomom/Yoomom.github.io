# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

"Youtube Videos Tool" is a personal, single-user application used by its owner to upload videos to their own YouTube channel and to read their own channel's analytics.

## Data accessed
When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests access to the owner's own YouTube account (upload videos, and read-only YouTube channel and analytics data).

## How data is used
This data is used only to perform the actions the owner asks for, such as uploading a video or showing channel analytics. It is not used for advertising and is never sold or shared with anyone.

## Storage
Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server and no database, and it collects no data from any other user.

## Google API Services
The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Revoking access
The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## Contact
Questions can be sent through the GitHub profile at https://github.com/Yoomom.
